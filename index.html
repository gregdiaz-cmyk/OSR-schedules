Gap Analysis & Recommendations

Zero coverage gaps Resolved
The 3-block system eliminates all zero-coverage windows. Every hour of every day has at least 5–7 reps on shift — far better than the current 1-person Sunday gaps.
TL coverage upgraded Resolved
3 TLs replace the current 2. Each block has a dedicated TL with clean 15-min overlap handoffs at 06:00, 14:00, and 22:00 PHT. No more TLs covering hours at the fringe of their shift.
